THE STORY IN NUMBERS (memorize for the demo)
  Hero: a Pune bakery owner; "today" = Friday 6 Nov 2026, 7:00 PM IST
        (injected clock; DEMO_MODE=true).

  Windows compared (completed orders, 3-6 p.m., Mon-Fri):
    26-30 Oct 2026: 100 orders, Rs 8,000 net sales
     2-6 Nov 2026:  70 orders, Rs 5,600 net sales
    Delta: -30% on both orders and sales.
    AOV: Rs 80 -> Rs 80 (flat - the decline is order COUNT).

  Per-order totals distribution (these sum EXACTLY to the window totals):
    Previous window: 30x Rs 60 + 30x Rs 80 + 20x Rs 90 + 20x Rs 100 = Rs 8,000
    Current window:  10x Rs 60 + 15x Rs 70 + 20x Rs 80 + 15x Rs 90 + 10x Rs 100 = Rs 5,600
  The seeder also adds: two context weeks (Oct 12-16, Oct 19-23 afternoons,
  plausible counts, no exact invariant), morning orders (8-11 a.m.) for both
  hero weeks, ONE pending order (Fri 17:30) and ONE canceled order (Wed 16:10)
  in the current window - both must stay OUT of completed totals.

MENU / SKUS (price / variable cost, in paise in the fixture)
  SKU-SAMOSA   Samosa            Rs 35 / cost Rs 15
  SKU-TEA      Tea               Rs 20 / cost Rs 10
  SKU-PASTRY   Veg pastry        Rs 40 / cost Rs 18
  SKU-SANDWICH Grilled sandwich  Rs 50 / cost Rs 22
  SKU-JUICE    Fresh lime juice  Rs 60 / cost Rs 25

THE OFFER
  Bundle: two samosas + one tea (2 x SKU-SAMOSA + 1 x SKU-TEA)
  Usual combined price: 2x35 + 20 = Rs 90
  Proposed price: Rs 70  ->  discount Rs 20 (22.2%)
  Variable cost: 2x15 + 10 = Rs 40
  Contribution: 70 - 40 = Rs 30
  Margin: 30/70 = 42.857%  (policy floor: 25%)
  Inventory: 60 samosas, 25 teas (plus other SKUs) -> capacity = min(60/2, 25) = 25
  Redemption cap: 20/day  ->  max discount exposure = 20 x 20 = Rs 400
  At cap: revenue Rs 1,400, cost Rs 800, contribution Rs 600;
          remaining stock: 20 samosas, 5 teas.

CUSTOMERS + CONSENT (demo CRM; never inferred from payments)
  50 synthetic customers (non-deliverable demo ids "demo-block-40xx").
  40 active SMS consents (sources: "in-store signup card" / "QR opt-in at counter").
  5 revoked, 5 never captured -> 40 eligible / 10 excluded.

SIMULATED RESULTS (what the demo shows at the end)
  10 unique redemption events (R-001..R-010), each 1 bundle @ Rs 70:
    revenue Rs 700 - variable cost Rs 400 = Rs 300 contribution
    discount given Rs 200. Cap 20 NOT reached (10 < 20).
  R-003 replayed -> no double count (idempotent).
  No open-rate, no causal lift, no acquisition claims. Ever.

OPPORTUNITY RULES (demo heuristic, not statistics)
  Both windows complete, >= 20 completed afternoon orders each, decline
  >= 10%, and inventory can physically support the bundle. Otherwise the API
  returns items: [] with a lowDataReason string - the UI shows that honestly.

CLOCK RULES
  DEMO_MODE=true (default) -> now = fixture clock. DEMO_NOW=<iso> overrides.
  DEMO_MODE=false -> real wall clock (only sensible on the right weekday/time).
  Expiry: offer valid until the NEXT weekday (Mon-Fri) 18:00 IST.
