MERCHANT SAATHI - TEAM CONTEXT PACK
01-PROJECT-OVERVIEW
================================================================================

THE PITCH (one sentence)
"A daily growth partner that tells a small merchant which opportunity deserves
attention, proves the numbers behind it, and turns the owner's decision into a
trackable campaign."

THE PROBLEM (from our slide deck)
Our hero user is a Pune bakery owner (fictional). Between the two most recent
complete Mon-Fri windows, completed 3-6 p.m. orders fell from 100 to 70 (-30%)
and net sales from Rs 8,000 to Rs 5,600. Average order value stayed flat at
Rs 80 - so the problem is order COUNT, not basket size. A dashboard alone just
shows the decline; the owner still has to figure out WHAT to do, whether it is
affordable, what to say to customers, and whether it worked.

THE SOLUTION (our hero workflow)
  1. Owner sees the decline with evidence (windows, counts, definitions).
  2. Owner asks for a margin-safe idea: "two samosas + one tea" at Rs 70
     (usual combined Rs 90), capped at 20 redemptions/day.
  3. The app computes - in code, not AI - contribution (Rs 30/bundle),
     margin (42.9% vs a 25% floor), stock capacity (25 bundles), and maximum
     discount exposure (Rs 400). An unsafe price (Rs 50 -> 20% margin) is
     visibly REJECTED before anything can be queued.
  4. The AI drafts the explanation and the customer message FROM those
     verified numbers only. It cannot change money, stock or consent.
  5. Owner approves the EXACT version (hash-bound). Any later change
     invalidates the approval.
  6. 40 consented demo customers enter a SIMULATED outbox. Nothing is sent.
  7. Ten simulated redemption events are applied: Rs 700 revenue
     - Rs 400 variable cost = Rs 300 contribution toward fixed costs.
     Every screen labels this "Simulated campaign outcomes".

WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT (USP)
  - Evidence receipt: every number links to a metric id, definition, period
    and source. There is a dedicated evidence drawer on the screens.
  - The product can say "insufficient data" and REFUSES unprofitable offers.
  - Honest labels everywhere: synthetic data, injected clock, AI mode
    (fixture/live/fallback), simulated outcomes. Judges can trust the demo.

WHAT IS REAL VS SIMULATED (memorize this for the demo)
  REAL (computed in code):   sales metrics, offer economics, capacity,
                             consent filtering, approval binding, idempotency,
                             cap enforcement, results arithmetic.
  SIMULATED (labeled):       the bakery dataset (fixture bakery-v1),
                             the customer list and consents (50 fake IDs),
                             message delivery (outbox, never sends),
                             redemptions (10 fixed fixture events),
                             the clock (pinned to Fri 6 Nov 2026, 7:00 PM IST).
  AI:                        drafts explanation + copy only, from verified
                             facts; can be fixture (default) or live Z.ai.

BUSINESS MODEL HYPOTHESIS (to validate, not to claim)
Monthly subscription per active shop covering recurring sales reviews, offer
preparation with evidence checks, and campaign reporting. Pricing stays open
until 3-5 Pune bakery owners have tried the workflow (our proposed pilot).

ONE-LINE TECH SUMMARY
Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind app; SQLite database; deterministic business
logic in tested pure functions; a bounded AI adapter (fixture or live Z.ai);
Vitest + Playwright tests. Single-process prototype - local judging is the
guaranteed deployment path.
