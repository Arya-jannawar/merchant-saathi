MERCHANT SAATHI - TEAM CONTEXT PACK
01-PROJECT-OVERVIEW
================================================================================

THE PITCH (one sentence)
"Merchant Saathi is an AI business copilot for every Paytm merchant: it helps
them GROW with evidence-backed offers, MANAGE operations with restock
intelligence, and SERVE customers with consent-first campaigns - proving every
number on the way."

THE PROBLEM STATEMENT WE ANSWER
"Build scalable AI-led solutions that help Paytm merchants grow their
business, manage operations, and serve customers better. Think beyond
payments, identify opportunities where AI can become a trusted business
copilot for millions of merchants."

Our answer maps one-to-one onto the three verbs:

  PILLAR 1 - GROW (the original hero loop)
    A Pune bakery owner's completed 3-6 p.m. orders fell from 100 to 70 (-30%)
    between two complete Mon-Fri windows; net sales Rs 8,000 -> Rs 5,600; AOV
    flat at Rs 80 (order COUNT, not basket size). A dashboard alone just shows
    the decline. The copilot answers, in order:
      1. What changed?          -> ONE opportunity with evidence receipts
      2. What should I do?      -> a capped offer (two samosas + one tea @ 70)
      3. Can I afford it?       -> code computes margin/capacity/exposure;
                                   unsafe prices are visibly REJECTED
      4. What do I tell people? -> AI drafts copy from the verified numbers
                                   only (English/Hindi)
      5. Did it work?           -> hash-bound approval -> SIMULATED outbox ->
                                   10 idempotent redemption events ->
                                   Rs 700 - Rs 400 = Rs 300 contribution
    The loop is domain-aware: register a kirana store and the same audited
    math prices "Rice + dal family pack" instead.

  PILLAR 2 - MANAGE OPERATIONS (the "beyond payments" answer)
    The Operations tab (/merchant/operations) computes, from real rows:
      - Run-rate per SKU (units sold/day across the current window)
      - Days of cover per SKU (stock / run-rate), color-coded
        Healthy / Low / Critical / Out
      - Campaign draw-down (a queued offer still consumes bundle components;
        projected stock after the campaign is pre-computed)
      - A costed restock plan targeting 14 days of cover
    Advisory by design: the copilot computes and narrates; it NEVER mutates
    inventory or orders stock automatically. The owner decides.

  PILLAR 3 - SERVE CUSTOMERS BETTER
    Consent health at a glance (active / revoked / never captured) plus
    redemption champions - which consented customers actually showed up.
    Serving better = serving only what customers agreed to, then rewarding
    the regulars. Campaigns reach only active-consent customers, always.

WHY JUDGES BELIEVE IT (the USP)
  - Evidence receipts: every number links to a metric id, definition, window
    and source. There is a dedicated evidence drawer.
  - The platform can say "insufficient evidence" and REFUSES unprofitable
    offers - visibly, with the approve button disabled.
  - Honest labels everywhere: synthetic data, injected clock, AI mode
    (fixture/live/fallback), simulated outcomes. Judges can trust the demo.

WHAT IS REAL VS SIMULATED (memorize this for the demo)
  REAL (computed in tested code):   sales metrics, offer economics, capacity,
                                    consent filtering, approval binding,
                                    idempotency, cap enforcement, results
                                    arithmetic, run-rates, restock plan
  SIMULATED (labeled):              the domain datasets (fixtures), the
                                    customer list and consents, message
                                    delivery (outbox, never sends), the
                                    redemption events, the clock (pinned to
                                    Fri 6 Nov 2026, 7:00 PM IST)
  AI:                               drafts explanation + customer copy only,
                                    from verified facts - fixture mode
                                    (default, no credentials) or live Z.ai.
                                    Chat answers come from a "fact book"
                                    built from the same verified numbers.

THE PLATFORM (how users arrive)
  New sessions land on a registration hero: pick a business name, a domain
  (Bakery / Cafe / Kirana / Salon / Mobile / Juice / Auto / Coaching), and a
  mascot buddy (Byte, Firewall, Java, Crypto, Pixel, Glitch). Every domain
  ships themed SKUs/bundles over the SAME audited economics. Up to 6
  businesses per session; the mascot buddy interacts (hover emotions, petting,
  signature-move showcase, sleep cycle) and guides a spotlight tour.

BUSINESS MODEL HYPOTHESIS (to validate, not to claim)
  Monthly subscription per active shop covering recurring sales reviews, offer
  preparation with evidence checks, campaign reporting, and operations
  intelligence. Pricing stays open until 3-5 Pune merchants have tried the
  workflow (our proposed pilot).

ONE-LINE TECH SUMMARY
  Next.js 14 + TypeScript strict + Tailwind; SQLite via better-sqlite3;
  deterministic business logic in tested pure functions (95 tests); a bounded
  AI adapter (fixture or live Z.ai); hand-rolled SVG/CSS visualizations and a
  synthesized Web Audio sound kit (zero chart/animation/audio dependencies);
  Vitest + Playwright. Single-process prototype - local judging is the
  guaranteed deployment path.
