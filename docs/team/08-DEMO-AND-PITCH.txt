JUDGE Q&A (the answers that score)
  Q: Where do the numbers come from?
  A: One fixture per domain (JSON), a deterministic seeder, and a
     reconciliation script (fixtures:verify) that checks every number against
     freshly seeded rows - it caught a real migration bug during development
     and we fixed the migration.

  Q: Is the AI doing the math?
  A: Never. All economics run in pure TypeScript functions (computeOffer).
     The AI drafts prose from the computed facts; its output is schema-checked
     and its rupee amounts are validated against the facts - violations fall
     back to a labeled template.

  Q: Are messages really sent?
  A: Never. The outbox is simulated and labeled on every screen; demo
     customer ids are non-deliverable by design.

  Q: How does this scale to millions of merchants?
  A: Session-isolated multi-tenancy (every query filters by merchant id),
     domain-versioned fixtures, pure domain functions with no HTTP or React
     in them, and a repository seam around SQLite - swap to managed Postgres
     and the copilot logic is unchanged. Chat answers are grounded per
     merchant via the same fact pack the screens use.

  Q: What if the model is down or returns nonsense?
  A: 15s timeout, one retry, Zod validation, evidence-id allowlist, rupee
     guard - any failure falls back to the labeled fixture draft / fact book.
     The product never depends on the model being smart.

  Q: Why does it refuse to recommend anything on the thin dataset?
  A: Because that is the honest answer. Eligibility is a demo heuristic and
     the platform states it instead of guessing. Trust beats theater.
