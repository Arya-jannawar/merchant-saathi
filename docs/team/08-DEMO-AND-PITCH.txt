LIKELY QUESTIONS + SHORT ANSWERS
  Q: Why is this AI at all?
  A: The model translates verified business facts into accessible explanation
     and customer-ready copy. Arithmetic and authority stay in tested code -
     that's deliberate: proof, not a black box.
  Q: Where does the data come from?
  A: A transparent synthetic fixture today; authorized merchant/POS exports
     and platform integrations later. Payment records alone never imply SKU
     costs, inventory or messaging consent - consent has its own register.
  Q: How do you prove growth?
  A: We don't - with fixtures you can't. We prove a usable action loop, correct
     economics, and honest measurement; lift belongs to a controlled pilot.
  Q: What happens if stock or consent changes after approval?
  A: Execution rechecks both and refuses stale state (422) - show the audit
     trail on the campaign page.
  Q: Why Rs 50 fails but Rs 70 passes?
  A: Rs 50 leaves Rs 10 after the Rs 40 variable cost = 20% margin, below her
     25% floor. Rs 70 leaves Rs 30 = 42.9%. The floor is her policy, editable.
  Q: Can it scale / is it production-ready?
  A: It's a single-process prototype: SQLite + demo sessions. The next
     engineering steps are managed Postgres, durable jobs, real auth and
     rate limits - we don't claim they're built.
  Q: Is the AI live?
  A: Label on screen: fixture mode (deterministic, no credentials) unless we
     verified a live call - then the badge says live and the fallback banner
     explains any failure honestly.
